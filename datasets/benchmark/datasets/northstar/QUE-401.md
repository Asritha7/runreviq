# Worker queue backlog

Fictional benchmark only. No real infrastructure or customer data.

## Situation

The fictional Dispatch service has a growing task backlog and older messages despite normal producer throughput. Record queue depth, oldest message age and worker completion rate. A large queue alone does not distinguish slow workers from repeated failures.

## Investigation

Compare task arrival rate with worker completion rate and inspect retry counts. Identify whether a small group of poison messages repeatedly fails. Review the downstream dependency error rate before adding more workers and amplifying load.

## Controlled response

With queue-owner approval, quarantine identified poison messages while preserving their identifiers for investigation. Increase worker concurrency only if downstream capacity is available. Do not purge the queue or acknowledge unfinished tasks to hide the backlog.

## Verification

Recovery requires the oldest message age to decrease while successful completions exceed arrivals. Confirm that quarantined work has an owner and a replay plan. This procedure does not specify message ordering guarantees or exactly-once delivery.
